# M4 missing-data priorities after Phase 1B

These are gaps in the current descriptive foundation, ranked for the first behavioural M4 model. No substitute series was silently introduced.

## P0-next

- Policy-rate history and gross reserves from BoM: the official endpoints are identified but currently return no-result responses for tested ranges.
- Chinese demand/activity proxy: required for the dominant export-market channel.

## P1-next

- FDI and current account/BOP: important for mining-finance and external sustainability channels.
- International copper/coal/oil benchmarks: useful for separating Mongolian export quantity from world-price movements.

## Later

- More detailed project-level production/capex schedules, labour-market microstructure, and disaggregated commodity grades.

Bank of Mongolia and international benchmark connections remain intentionally deferred. The first model should not treat those gaps as zero or fill them with an unproven substitute.
